1b Map frame — origin & conveyor axis

Define the world frame on the mesh: double-click the origin (centre of the 8×11 board under basler_1), then double-click a point along the conveyor (a far board) to set the axis. World +Y runs along the conveyor, +X across. The three boards should then read a near-constant X.

Frame: origin (0,0), +Y = mesh X.

2 Calibrate — pick matching points

Double-click a feature on the 3D conveyor (left) and the same feature in the camera frame (right) — that is one correspondence. Orbit the map with drag, zoom with the wheel. Four pairs is the minimum; a fifth is the first that gives a real error.
