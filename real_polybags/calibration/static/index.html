    4 Belt plane — extrinsics
    
    
    
      Extrinsics live on their own page. They are a
      different job from this one: intrinsics describe the lens and can be
      measured at a desk, while extrinsics describe where the camera is bolted
      and need the rig in its final state. Doing all four lenses first, then
      every pose in one sitting against a single board placement, is the order
      that actually works — and the rig page is built around it.
    
    
    
    
      It also holds the anticipated measurements: the height
      each camera is expected to sit at, and the origin offsets to be taken with
      a tape. Recorded before the solve, they turn each result into a comparison
      instead of a number to squint at.
    
    
    
    
      
    
    
  
    

  
  
    
    
    5 Belt map
    
    
    
      The top-down metric map of the conveyor, built from every saved
      calibration. This is the artefact the MOT stage consumes: a bag at
      (X, Y) millimetres here is the same bag regardless of which camera saw
      it. It also measures whether the cameras overlap — a
      question this rig has never settled.
